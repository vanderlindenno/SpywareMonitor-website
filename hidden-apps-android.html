How to find hidden apps on Android

An app may not appear on the home screen, and an unfamiliar name can be hard to interpret. Review the full installed-app list and its permissions, then consider the context before taking action.

Hidden does not automatically mean harmful. Some system and accessibility apps have technical names or no launcher icon by design. Confirm the app’s publisher and purpose before deciding whether it is suspicious.

What “hidden app” can mean

People may use “hidden app” to describe an app whose icon is not shown in the app drawer, an app with a confusing system-like name, or an app that runs in the background. Android system components and legitimate services can match some of these descriptions too.

Focus on what the app can access, who published it, why it is on the phone and whether its permissions make sense for its stated purpose.

Where to review apps on Android

  1. Open Android Settings and find Apps. Choose the option that shows all installed apps. The wording and location vary by Android version and phone manufacturer.
  2. Look for unfamiliar entries. Check the name, app details, publisher and install source when available. Do not rely on the icon alone.
  3. Review special access and permissions. Look at Accessibility, Notification access, Device admin apps, Location and permission manager. Check whether an app has access that is unusual for its purpose.
  4. Check for managed or work profiles. Apps installed in a separate profile may not appear in the personal profile’s app list. A work-managed phone may also be governed by an organisation.
  5. Use a second opinion if uncertain. Ask a trusted technical support person or safety organisation to help interpret what you find.

Look at access

Accessibility or notification access can be powerful. It can also be needed for legitimate assistive or device features.

Check install source

An app installed from outside an app store may deserve closer review, but this alone does not prove malicious intent.

Before disabling or removing an app

If you are concerned that another person may have installed the app to monitor you, consider your safety before changing anything. Removal or permission changes could alert them. If safe, note the app name and its access. Get support from a device you believe is not monitored before making changes.

Frequently asked questions

Can Android apps hide their icon?

Some apps can be configured not to show a launcher icon, and some system components do not have one. The absence of an icon is a clue to inspect the app, not proof that it is spyware.

Can I see all installed apps in Android settings?

Settings usually provides a full app list for the current profile, but menu labels vary. Work profiles, managed devices and rooted phones can change what is visible to a normal app.

Should I force-stop or disable a hidden app?

Not before considering safety and the app’s role. Disabling or removing it can cause problems or alert a person who installed it. Seek advice if you may be at risk.
