Full text of the video

0:00
Is someone secretly watching your phone? SpywareMonitor helps you find out.
0:06
Open the app, and it checks your phone in a few seconds. Nothing leaves your phone: the app has no internet access.
0:15
Orange means an app has access that could be misused. Often it's harmless. Red means it's known spyware.
0:23
Tap Apps to see which app was found, and why. For example: it can read your screen, and it hides its icon.
0:32
Don't remove it straight away: that can warn the person who put it there. Tap What to do now.
0:38
You get steps in a safe order. First keep evidence, then get help, from a phone that isn't being watched.
0:46
In the Safety tab, pick what fits: an app, someone unlocking your phone, unsafe settings, or feeling watched.
0:55
With Pro, the app checks by itself, every hour, and keeps a log of when your phone was unlocked.
1:02
You can disguise it as Weather or Notes, and lock it with a PIN.
1:07
Need to leave quickly? Tap the arrow at the top right.
1:11
SpywareMonitor. No internet, no ads. On Google Play from mid-November.

The video was made before the launch; SpywareMonitor is on Google Play now, in 10 languages. Since then, the app checks several times a day instead of every hour, and What to do now is called Safe steps.
