1. What the app does

SpywareMonitor looks at the apps and settings on the phone it is installed on and shows which of them could be used to watch you, such as known stalkerware, hidden apps, apps that can read your screen or notifications, and settings that make spying easier. The free version checks your phone when you open the app. SpywareMonitor Pro adds automatic checks in the background, history with an unlock log, an evidence report, a disguised app icon and a PIN lock.

2. Use it only on your own phone

You may use SpywareMonitor on a phone you own, or on a phone you are allowed to check, for example a work phone with your employer's permission. Do not use the app, or anything it shows you, to watch, track or control another person. That may be against the law, and it is the opposite of what the app is made for.

3. No guarantee

No app can find every kind of spying. Spyware changes, new apps appear, and some kinds of surveillance leave no trace on the phone at all, for example a shared account, location sharing, a tracker in a bag or access to your cloud backups. So:

  • If SpywareMonitor finds nothing, that does not prove that nobody is watching you.
  • If SpywareMonitor marks an app as worth a look, that does not prove that the app is used to spy on you. Many ordinary apps need the same access.
  • The list of known stalkerware is built into the app and updated through Google Play. It may not yet include the newest apps.

Use what the app shows as a starting point, not as a final answer.

4. Your safety comes first

If someone is watching you, removing an app or changing a setting can tell that person that you have noticed. Before you change anything, think about whether that could put you at risk, and consider talking to someone you trust, a support service or the police. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in Norway and the rest of Europe). SpywareMonitor is a tool, not a replacement for professional help or legal advice.
